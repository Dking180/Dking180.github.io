01 / THE STORYTELLER

Behind the stories.

I am Darshan R H, known creatively as D'king — an independent writer and filmmaker passionate about cinema and storytelling.

My creative world explores the connection between emotions, human choices, faith, relationships and consequences.

I believe every story deserves an identity, every character deserves a purpose, and every frame should make the audience feel something.

My vision: Stories that stay beyond the screen.
